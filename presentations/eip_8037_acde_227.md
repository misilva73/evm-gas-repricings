# EIP-8037: State Creation Gas Cost Increase

## ACDE \#227

### Maria

#### January 5th, 2026

[Slides](https://notes.ethereum.org/@misilva/S15O_Cpzbg)

---

## Initial proposal in EIP-8037

- One-time increase in state creation costs (~10x)
- Assuming a block limit of 300M gas
- Contract creation costs are metered separately -> EIP-7825 limit

---

## Updates

- Incorporated Lukasz idea to have a dynamic cost based on the current gas limit
  - There will be smaller cost increases as the gas limit raises
- All state creation cost are metered separately
  - Still avoids EIP-7825 limit on contract creation
  - Plus avoids throughput losses from higher gas costs for state creation

:::warning
Francesco has a [draft spec](https://github.com/ethereum/execution-specs/compare/forks/osaka...fradamt:execution-specs:state-growth) for this new version!
:::
